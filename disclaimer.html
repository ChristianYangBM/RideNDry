4. Product Installation, Use & Removal

To install the Product, lay the cover flat across the dash. Loop the cord around upright tweeters in the corners of the dash and windshield. If tweeters lay flat, place the hook in the corner to secure the hold. Attach hooks on each side of the dash between the dash and door frame. Then attach all hooks into the defrost vents and secure the Product around the sides of the dashboard, adjusting bungee cords for a secure fit.

For more information about proper installation, please view the installation videos on the manufacturer’s website at ridendry.com.

Before operating the vehicle with the installed Product, ensure that all gear shifts, steering column arms, and vehicle operating areas are not restricted by the Product. If head lamps and wipers will be used, place them in the “on” position before covers are placed on steering arms. Do not place, attach, cover, or obstruct airbag openings with the Product.

During use, all vehicle vents should be open and placed on a low defrost setting to help avoid fogging of the plastic panels.

When removing the Product, do not tug, pull, or force the cover off the dashboard. Gently remove the hooks from their positions to avoid damage to the Product and vehicle.

The Product is meant for use only in emergency rain conditions or when off-roading in a vehicle on which the doors have been removed. Prolonged use for extended driving periods could lead to dashboard overheating and vehicle damage.

The Product is not machine washable and should not be dry cleaned. Do not clean it while installed. Remove it, place it on a flat surface, clean it with warm water and mild soap, and hang it to dry.
